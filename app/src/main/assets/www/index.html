Setup

One-time TV pairing, plus app launch configs.

1. TV address

Find this on your Vizio TV: Menu → Network → Network Connection → (your WiFi) → IP Address.

2. Pair with the TV

Starts a pairing request — a PIN will appear on the TV screen.

3. App launch configs

There's no universal way to tell a streaming app to jump to a specific episode, so this app uses a simple trick: open the app on the TV once (any screen), then capture it here. Quick Launch will reopen the app to that same captured state. Capture again any time you want to refresh it.
